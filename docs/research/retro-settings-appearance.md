# Modern retro appearance for Simple STT Settings

Use a cobalt page header, a cool neutral form surface, and fine rectangular edges. Keep the current task navigation and use the same readable system type for English and Arabic labels. The header, field edges, and short status labels carry the retro influence. Forms get their hierarchy from headings, alignment, and spacing.

## Design references

- Jordan Scales’s 98.css shows how a strong title strip, engraved group borders, and inset input edges distinguish the parts of a dialog. Its examples retain labeled native HTML controls. For Simple STT, use a single cobalt strip for the page heading, thin rules between related rows, and a one-pixel inset or pressed edge where it reinforces a control’s state. Keep the native window frame. [98.css components](https://jdan.github.io/98.css/)
- Microsoft’s Win32 guidance recommends short tips near their anchor and puts essential information in visible labels or adjacent text. Apply the same economy to shortcut hints, model labels, and feedback. A compact status line can expose real state, such as a model download or a pending save. [Win32 Tooltips and Infotips](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/uxguide/ctrl-tooltips-and-infotips)
- Lexaloffle’s PICO-8 documents a repeating two-color 4×4 fill. Use a small, static two-tone tile at the trailing edge of the header to suggest a low-resolution display. Give the title, inputs, and status text solid backdrops. Teenage Engineering’s EP–133 pairs its segmented display with distinct controls, a precedent for reserving this treatment for the framing of an audio tool. [PICO-8 manual](https://www.lexaloffle.com/dl/docs/pico-8_manual.html), [Teenage Engineering EP–133](https://teenage.engineering/products/ep-133)

## Palette and verification

A starting light palette pairs white text with cobalt `#2343D2` at 7.49:1. On a `#EDF0FA` surface, `#19223F` body text gives 13.75:1, `#555E79` secondary text gives 5.65:1, and a `#6B76A1` control edge gives 3.90:1. These are calculated ratios for flat sRGB colors. Measure the actual rendered combinations in each theme. WCAG 2.2 AA calls for at least 4.5:1 for normal text and at least 3:1 for the visual information needed to identify controls and their states, subject to the criterion’s exceptions. [WCAG text contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum), [WCAG non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#non-text-contrast), [WCAG contrast ratio calculation](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#dfn-contrast-ratio)

- Tab through the sidebar and a form in both themes. Give keyboard focus a visible outline distinct from the selected page and pressed controls. Use text or a clear shape alongside color for meaningful states. Check OS forced colors with actual borders and a system-colored focus outline because forced colors can remove shadows. [WCAG focus visibility](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#focus-visible), [WCAG use of color](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#use-of-color), [W3C forced colors](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-adjust-1/#forced-colors-properties)
- Open a narrow pane and enlarge the text in English and Arabic. Labels and values should wrap with every setting still reachable, and the patterned edge and directional decorations should follow the writing direction. Keep small visible glyphs inside targets of at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels as the default. Use the existing narrow Arabic fixture for screenshot checks. [WCAG reflow](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#reflow), [WCAG target size](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#target-size-minimum), [Microsoft layout and RTL guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/uwp/design/globalizing/adjust-layout-and-fonts--and-support-rtl), [Simple STT testing matrix](../testing.md#browser-settings-coverage)
